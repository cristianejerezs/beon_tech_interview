# Five-analyst acceptance protocol

**Status: NOT RUN. No participants or outcomes are claimed.**

Prerequisites: a provider passing the golden corpus, corrected evidence/exports,
and completed authenticated accessibility review. Do not recruit through this test suite
or transmit participant questions, identities, recordings, or credentials externally.

Use five real volunteers, labelled A-E only. Explain optional reuse and turn both purposes
off for private testing. Use synthetic questions and the supplied historical dataset.
Give tasks without explaining the correct UI steps; assistance means the task did not
meet the unaided criterion. Record only pass/fail and de-identified usability observations.

1. **SC-001:** Ask the participant to compare EWR/JFK/LGA connectivity in 2013.
   Pass if they submit a clear question and identify that the report addresses it unaided.
2. **SC-004:** Ask them to narrow an existing report to one airport and June 2013.
   Pass if they use refinement and correctly explain the new active scope unaided.
3. **SC-006:** Ask them to locate a delay finding, explain the average's population
   and missing-value handling, and identify a historical-data limitation.
   Pass if all three are found and correctly explained unaided.
